Introduction

CyberConnect Podcast is an educational initiative focused on raising awareness about cybersecurity, digital laws, artificial intelligence risks, and emerging technological threats in Ghana.

Problem Statement

Cyber threats in Ghana continue to escalate, ranging from misinformation campaigns to unauthorized distribution of private content. Many citizens remain unaware of the legal implications under Ghana’s Cybersecurity Act 2020.

Recent cyber incidents affecting national infrastructure demonstrate the urgent need for digital awareness and proactive security measures.

Objectives

The primary objective of CyberConnect Podcast is to educate listeners about responsible digital behavior and cybersecurity awareness.

Significance

CyberConnect collaborates with experts and institutions such as Ghana’s Cybersecurity Authority, helping strengthen digital resilience.
